D.A.R.T (D3ll Athlete Records Tracker)

D.A.R.T. (Dell Athlete Record Tracker) is Liberia’s first digital sports CV network built to showcase talent with integrity. We bridge the gap between grassroots athletics and global scouting by replacing unverified stats with an official, cloud-connected pipeline for football, basketball, and kickball.

Track every goal, basket, and home run in one secure hub—fully backed by the coaches who know you best.
