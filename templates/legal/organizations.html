Version {{ version }}. Every Enterprise, Institution, Academy or Club account accepts these terms when it registers. They protect the athletes on your roster, many of whom are minors.

1. Verification

Your account and roster stay hidden until D.A.R.T. verifies your official document. Only names approved by D.A.R.T. decide which athletes join your roster. You must be authorized to act for the organization.

2. What you receive

Sport information only: name, photo, age, gender, nationality, height, weight, preferred foot, shirt number, positions, approved records and highlights. You never receive athletes' email addresses, ID documents, dates of birth or messages.

3. How you may use it

  • Only to manage, develop, select and celebrate your own athletes, and to report on your sports programme.
  • Never sell, rent or give roster data to anyone else, use it for advertising, or combine it with other data to profile athletes.
  • If an athlete doesn't play for you, remove them from your roster.

4. Security

  • Keep two-factor authentication on, never share the login, and give access only to staff who need it.
  • Store any exported roster securely and delete it when it is no longer needed.
  • Tell D.A.R.T. straight away if you think your account or an export was exposed.

4a. Games and line-ups (Organization Pro)

  • Enter each game's line-up no later than 5 minutes before kick-off; it then locks. List only real squad members with their real shirt numbers and positions.
  • Post true results and statistics only after the game. The D.A.R.T Admin compares both teams' line-ups and confirms them; false line-ups or results may lead to suspension and a report to the relevant federation or the Ministry of Youth & Sports.
  • The line-up PDF is for your own team management: keep it secure and don't publish it elsewhere, because it names minors.

4b. Your public page

Your verified organization page shows your details, ownership, address and map location, logo, official athletes and recent games. Keep them accurate, and declare your true ownership: Organization Pro prices depend on it.

5. Transparency and records

Athletes can see which organizations list them. D.A.R.T. logs roster views and exports and may review them to protect athletes.

6. Suspension

D.A.R.T. may suspend an organization that misuses data or breaks these terms, and may report serious misuse to the relevant authorities.

7. Ending

If you close your account, stop using any roster data you exported, except where the law requires you to keep it.
