In short: we keep official sports records that coaches approve. We show sport information so athletes can be discovered, never sell personal data, never show ID documents publicly, and only show your personal details (age, nationality, height, weight, preferred foot) to the people you choose. You also control whether your profile appears on Google.

1. Who we are

D.A.R.T. (D3ll Athlete Records Tracker) is a sports-records platform based in Monrovia, Liberia. We are responsible for the personal data described here.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: name, username, team/school, email (optional at registration; verified within 2 business days), password (stored only as a secure hash), role (including head / assistant coach position) and registration category.
  • Athlete profile: gender, age, date of birth, nationality, height, weight, preferred foot, shirt number and profile picture.
  • Identity verification: the ID or passport you upload, seen only by coaches of your team and the D.A.R.T Admin.
  • Sports records and highlights: game statistics, photos and videos you upload.
  • Organizations: organization details, ownership (government or private, local or international), address and map location, logo, contact person and an official document for verification.
  • Organization games (Organization Pro): opponent, competition, kick-off, venue and capacity, the line-up (players' names, shirt numbers and positions), results, cards, fouls, saves and standings, and who confirmed the line-ups.
  • Vendors and buyers: business or shop name, business type, address and map location of the shop (vendors), phone number, and the D.A.R.T Admin's verification decision and reason.
  • Payments: plan, amount, mobile-money phone number and transaction ID. We never ask for or store your mobile-money PIN or card numbers.
  • Coaches and match officials: mobile number (to tie the account to one person) and, for match officials, the kind of official, their organization and an ID / press / league card seen only by the D.A.R.T Admin.
  • Parent / guardian (athletes under 17): name, relationship and phone number, seen only by the athlete's coaches and the D.A.R.T Admin, never by the public or scouts.
  • D.A.R.T Market$: your listings and photos (location data is removed from photos), your cart and orders, your messages with buyers / sellers, and your phone number, which is shown to the people you trade with.
  • Messages: the message, who it was sent to (To, Cc and Bcc), replies and forwards, when it was edited, and whether you deleted it from your own inbox.
  • Highlights activity: likes, comments (and when they were edited), shares and bookmarks. For visitors without an account: the name they type, the type of device taken from the browser (for example "iPhone" or "Windows PC"), a random visitor cookie and a one-way fingerprint of the IP address (not the address itself), used only to let them edit their own comments and to stop abuse.
  • Live visitor count: a random session number and the time it was last seen, so the D.A.R.T Admin can see how many people are on D.A.R.T. right now. It contains no name or IP address and is deleted after 2 days.
  • Record verification history: who submitted, edited, approved, counter-signed or disputed each record, and when, with a fingerprint of the numbers at each step. Match officials' match reports.
  • Security logs: sign-in attempts, IP address and important actions (approvals, payments, roster exports) to protect accounts.

3. How we use it

  • To run your account and verify identities (coaches approve athletes and records; the D.A.R.T Admin verifies scouts and organizations).
  • To show approved records to scouts, organizations and visitors, according to your plan.
  • To process payments and prevent fraud.
  • To keep the service secure, including two-factor authentication for coaches, scouts, organizations and administrators.
  • To show ads to free users through Google AdSense (see the Cookie Policy).
  • To keep D.A.R.T. safe: photos and videos are checked against our content rules before anyone else can see them (see section 4a).

4. What others can see

  • Sport information: your name, team, gender, shirt number, approved records and approved highlights. Free accounts show their latest approved record in search; Premium shows more.
  • Personal details, only with your permission: your age, nationality, height, weight and preferred foot are shown only to the people you choose: Everyone, Coaches, scouts & organizations (verified and signed in), or Only me. You choose when you accept these terms and can change it any time in My Profile. Until you choose, they are hidden. Searches by these details only find athletes who allow the person searching to see them.
  • Never shown to other users: your email, password, ID document, date of birth, phone number (except to the people you trade with on Market$, and a coach's number to signed-in users), messages and payment details. Bcc recipients are never shown to the other recipients.
  • Read-only profiles: names across D.A.R.T. link to the person's or organization's read-only profile, which shows only what is described in this section and your privacy choices. The D.A.R.T Admin's profile is not linked.
  • Organizations and shops: a verified organization's page shows its details, ownership, address and map, and its recent games. A vendor's shop page shows its address and map pin.
  • Games: a game page shows the result and statistics. Line-ups (players' names, shirt numbers and positions) are shown publicly only from kick-off; players who are on D.A.R.T. are linked to their profiles.
  • Organizations with your team name on their verified roster see your sport information, approved records and approved highlights, and your personal details only if you allow coaches, scouts & organizations to see them. You can see which organizations list you on your profile.
  • Your records are safe: only approved records are shown to others, only coaches of your team can approve them, and every approval, payment and roster export is logged.

4a. Photo and video safety checks

  • New highlights are stored privately and are not visible to anyone else until approved. Rejected files are deleted straight away.
  • To spot content that breaks the rules (nudity, rude content, selfies, other sports), photos, profile pictures and still frames from videos may be checked automatically by an AI service (Anthropic). It receives only the image(s), the sport you chose and your caption, not your name or contact details, and is used only to run this check. A D.A.R.T Admin then reviews highlights before they are published.
  • We keep a record of rejected uploads (the reason, not the file) to apply the strike rules in the Terms of Use.

5. Google and other search engines

Verified athletes aged 18 or over can appear in Google search unless they switch it off in My Profile. Athletes under 18 (or whose age is unknown) only appear if they switch it on. Switching it off removes your page from our sitemap and asks search engines not to show it; search engines may take some time to update.

6. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with service providers that run D.A.R.T. (hosting, database and file storage, email, Google sign-in, Google AdSense, Google Maps for shop and organization maps and directions, and the AI safety check described in section 4a), with mobile-money providers to confirm payments, or when the law requires it. When a coach's licence is revoked for fraudulent record approvals, we send the relevant sports federation (LFA / LBA) the coach's name, team, number and the records concerned.

7. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, two-factor authentication for privileged accounts, sign-in rate limits, access controls by role and audit logs. No system is perfectly secure; please use a strong, unique password and keep your authenticator safe.

8. How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account in My Profile, we delete your account, records, registrations, highlights, profile picture and ID document. Payment and security records may be kept longer where the law requires. Messages you sent stay in the other people's inboxes until they delete them. A game line-up an organization entered keeps the player's name as typed, but the link to a deleted profile stops working. The record verification history (who approved or disputed what, and when) is kept permanently to protect the integrity of every athlete's records; after deletion it no longer shows your profile.

9. Your rights

You can view and correct your profile at any time, choose who sees your personal details, delete your account, switch Google listing on or off, and ask us for a copy of your data or to correct anything you can't change yourself. Contact us at the address below.

10. Children

Many athletes are high-school students. Under-18s are not listed on Google unless they choose to be, their personal details are hidden unless they choose otherwise, and every photo and video is safety-checked before it is shown. Organizations must only put real squad members in line-ups, which are shown only from kick-off and never show age, date of birth or contact details. Parents or guardians may contact us about a minor's account.

11. Changes

We will post updates here and change the date above. Important changes will be announced in the app.
