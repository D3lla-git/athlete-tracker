Essential (always on)

  • Session cookie: keeps you signed in and protects forms from forgery (CSRF). Secure and HTTP-only.
  • Offline storage: the app can keep a copy of your records form and unsent records on your device so you can work without data. It is cleared when another user signs in on the same device.
  • Preferences: your light/dark theme and whether a celebration was already shown.
  • Live visitor count: a random number inside the session cookie lets us count how many people are on D.A.R.T. right now. It is not used to track you on other websites.
  • Visitor cookie (dart_vid): if you like or comment on a highlight without an account, a random number (kept up to 1 year, HTTP-only) lets you edit or remove your own comments. Your typed name is remembered on your device only (local storage).
  • Upload notes: the app may keep notes about an upload in your browser for the current tab only (session storage).

Google Maps

Organization and shop pages may show a Google map. When the map loads, or when you tap "Directions", Google may set its own cookies and receives the location shown. See policies.google.com/privacy.

Advertising (free plans and visitors)

Free accounts and visitors may see ads served by Google AdSense, which uses cookies to show and measure ads. Premium users, coaches and administrators don't see ads. Learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and manage ad personalization at adssettings.google.com.

Google sign-in

If you choose "Continue with Google", Google sets its own cookies during sign-in.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies will stop sign-in from working.
