About the records

Records are submitted by athletes and approved by coaches registered on D.A.R.T. Each shared record and PDF links back to its live page so anyone can check it is genuine and current. D.A.R.T. is independent and is not an official body of any football, basketball or kickball association unless stated.

Report a problem

To report a false record, inappropriate photo or video, a wrong roster listing, or a security issue, email us at the address below with the page link. We review reports promptly and may remove content while we investigate.
