In short: be honest, keep your account secure, respect other people's data, and only upload records and media that are true and yours to share.

1. Accepting these terms

By creating an account or using D.A.R.T. you agree to these Terms and the Privacy Policy. If you are under 18, a parent or guardian should review them with you.

2. Accounts

  • Give true information and keep it up to date. One person or organization per account.
  • Keep your password and authenticator private. You are responsible for activity on your account.
  • Coaches, scouts, organizations and administrators must use two-factor authentication.

3. Records

  • Upload only facts. Only submit games and statistics that really happened. Coaches review every record and, when they reject one, say why: Fake record, Inaccurate record (correct it and resubmit), or Mistakenly rejected (the coach's error, which removes any warning).
  • Fake records: the first is a warning, the second a final warning, and the third suspends your account. Deleting a rejected record does not remove the warning. A D.A.R.T. Super Admin can review a suspension if you think it is a mistake.
  • Only approved records are shown to others. Your personal details (age, nationality, height, weight, preferred foot) are shown only to the people you choose; see the Privacy Policy.

3a. Photos and videos: content rules

D.A.R.T. only accepts football, basketball and kickball action. Anything else is not allowed into D.A.R.T.

  • Photos must show you in action on the pitch or court (kicking, shooting, dribbling, saving, defending). No selfies, mirror pictures, posed portraits, party or street pictures.
  • Videos must be a direct highlight of you playing (1 minute at most). Edit or trim your video to the action before uploading. Distracted videos (vlogs, talking to the camera, crowds, long clips with little action) are not allowed.
  • Never allowed: nudity, sexual, rude, offensive or violent content, in highlights or profile pictures.
  • Every highlight is safety-checked (automatically and by a D.A.R.T. Super Admin) and only appears on your profile once approved. Files that break these rules are refused and deleted.
  • Strikes: each refused upload is a strike. {{ media_strike_limit }} strikes block photo and video uploads. Nudity or sexual content blocks uploads immediately and may lead to account suspension and, where the law requires, a report to the authorities.
  • Only upload media you have the right to share and that respects the people in it. You keep ownership and give D.A.R.T. permission to store and display approved media on the service and in shared links.

4. Premium plans and payments

  • Prices are shown in US dollars on the Pricing page. Athlete prices depend on your verified registration category.
  • Each payment gives 30 days. Plans do not renew automatically; you return to the free plan when they end and keep your records.
  • Mobile-money payments start when D.A.R.T. confirms the transaction. Submitting false payment details is fraud and leads to suspension.
  • While a Premium athlete plan is active, your category can't be changed, because prices depend on it.
  • Refunds: if you paid by mistake, paid twice, or Premium could not be activated, contact us within 7 days and we will refund or correct it.

5. Acceptable use

Don't: copy or scrape data in bulk, contact athletes for anything other than legitimate sport or education purposes, harass anyone, impersonate others, try to break security, or use data from D.A.R.T. to sell, advertise to, or profile athletes outside sport.

6. Organizations and scouts

Organizations also agree to the Organization Data Use Terms. Scouts must use athlete information only for genuine recruitment and must follow child-safeguarding practices when contacting minors (for example, through their school, club or guardian).

7. Suspension

We may suspend or close accounts that break these Terms or put others at risk, and remove content that is false or inappropriate.

8. Liability

We work hard to keep records accurate and the service available, but records come from athletes and coaches, and the service is provided "as is". To the extent the law allows, D.A.R.T. is not liable for indirect losses, or for decisions others make based on records.

9. Law

These Terms are governed by the laws of the Republic of Liberia. We will try to resolve any dispute informally first; contact us below.
