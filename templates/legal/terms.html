In short: be honest, keep your account secure, respect other people's data, and only upload records and media that are true and yours to share.

1. Accepting these terms

By creating an account or using D.A.R.T. you agree to these Terms and the Privacy Policy. If you are under 18, a parent or guardian should review them with you.

2. Accounts

  • Give true information and keep it up to date. One person or organization per account.
  • Keep your password and authenticator private. You are responsible for activity on your account.
  • Coaches, scouts, organizations, match officials and administrators must use two-factor authentication.
  • Athletes under 17 must give a parent / guardian contact and confirm that a parent, guardian or coach helps manage their D.A.R.T. page. Scouts must contact young athletes through their parent / guardian, coach, school or club.
  • Coaches and match officials must tie their account to their own Liberian mobile number (Orange or Lonestar). One number per account; it can only be changed through the D.A.R.T Admin.

3. Records

  • Upload only facts. Only submit games and statistics that really happened. Coaches review every record and, when they reject one, say why: Fake record, Inaccurate record (correct it and resubmit), or Mistakenly rejected (the coach's error, which removes any warning).
  • Fake records: the first is a warning, the second a final warning, and the third suspends your account. Deleting a rejected record does not remove the warning. A D.A.R.T Admin can review a suspension if you think it is a mistake.
  • Two independent sign-offs: a record is shown to others only after your coach approves it and someone independent counter-signs it (see 3b).
  • Only approved records are shown to others. Your personal details (age, nationality, height, weight, preferred foot) are shown only to the people you choose; see the Privacy Policy.

3a. Photos and videos: content rules

D.A.R.T. only accepts football, basketball and kickball action. Anything else is not allowed into D.A.R.T.

  • Photos must show you in action on the pitch or court (kicking, shooting, dribbling, saving, defending). No selfies, mirror pictures, posed portraits, party or street pictures.
  • Videos must be a direct highlight of you playing (1 minute at most). Edit or trim your video to the action before uploading. Distracted videos (vlogs, talking to the camera, crowds, long clips with little action) are not allowed.
  • Never allowed: nudity, sexual, rude, offensive or violent content, in highlights or profile pictures.
  • Every highlight is safety-checked (automatically and by a D.A.R.T Admin) and only appears on your profile once approved. Files that break these rules are refused and deleted.
  • Strikes: each refused upload is a strike. {{ media_strike_limit }} strikes block photo and video uploads. Nudity or sexual content blocks uploads immediately and may lead to account suspension and, where the law requires, a report to the authorities.
  • Only upload media you have the right to share and that respects the people in it. You keep ownership and give D.A.R.T. permission to store and display approved media on the service and in shared links.

3b. Record verification and coach integrity

  • Counter-signature. After the athlete's coach approves a record, the opposing team's coach (when they are on D.A.R.T.), otherwise a verified match official (sports journalist, league secretary, school sports coordinator, community youth leader or referee), otherwise the D.A.R.T Admin must confirm it. Nobody may counter-sign for a team they are tied to.
  • Disputes. If the counter-signer, or a match official's report of the same game, shows different numbers, the record goes to the D.A.R.T Admin's Dispute Queue and stays off the athlete's profile until a decision is made. Disputed records can't be edited.
  • Unusual numbers need proof. A game far above the athlete's usual (for example 4 to 45 points in basketball) or extreme for any single game (for example 6 home runs in kickball) is held until a match sheet or a video of the game is attached.
  • Permanent history. Every submission, edit, approval, counter-signature, dispute and decision is recorded permanently with the name of the person who made it, in a tamper-evident history that can't be edited or deleted, including after a record or account is deleted.
  • Coaches: fraud findings. When the D.A.R.T Admin finds that a coach approved a fake record, the coach receives a fraud finding. If your account is flagged twice for fraudulent stat approval, your licence to use D.A.R.T. is permanently revoked, and the Liberia Football Association (LFA) / Liberia Basketball Association (LBA) (or the relevant federation) will be formally notified.
  • Match officials must only confirm or report what really happened. False confirmations or reports lead to permanent removal and may be reported to the federation and the official's organization.
  • Offering or accepting money, gifts or favours to approve, counter-sign or report false statistics is bribery and breaks these Terms.

3c. D.A.R.T Market$ and Predictions

  • Sports gear only. D.A.R.T Market$ is for football, basketball and kickball gear (boots, basketball sneakers, kickball skips, sports socks, jerseys, balls, protection, equipment). Every listing, and every change to it, is checked by the D.A.R.T Admin before it is shown. Counterfeit, stolen, unsafe or non-sports items are refused and may lead to a ban.
  • D.A.R.T. is not part of the sale. Buyers and sellers chat in the app, see each other's phone number and pay each other directly (off D.A.R.T.). D.A.R.T. does not hold money, guarantee items or handle refunds. Meet in a public place, check the item before paying, and never pay in advance to someone you have not met.
  • Young athletes: athletes under 18 can browse D.A.R.T Market$ but cannot sell or message sellers; a parent, guardian or coach buys for them.
  • Your phone number is shown to the person you are buying from or selling to. Report scams or wrong listings with the Report button.
  • Predictions are a free game with points only: no money can be paid or won. Questions are only about athletes aged 18 or over, and athletes and match officials do not take part.

4. Premium plans and payments

  • Prices are shown in US dollars on the Pricing page. Athlete prices depend on your verified registration category.
  • Each payment gives 30 days. Plans do not renew automatically; you return to the free plan when they end and keep your records.
  • Mobile-money payments start when D.A.R.T. confirms the transaction. Submitting false payment details is fraud and leads to suspension.
  • While a Premium athlete plan is active, your category can't be changed, because prices depend on it.
  • Refunds: if you paid by mistake, paid twice, or Premium could not be activated, contact us within 7 days and we will refund or correct it.

5. Acceptable use

Don't: copy or scrape data in bulk, contact athletes for anything other than legitimate sport or education purposes, harass anyone, impersonate others, try to break security, or use data from D.A.R.T. to sell, advertise to, or profile athletes outside sport.

6. Organizations and scouts

Organizations also agree to the Organization Data Use Terms. Scouts must use athlete information only for genuine recruitment and must follow child-safeguarding practices when contacting minors (for example, through their school, club or guardian).

7. Suspension

We may suspend or close accounts that break these Terms or put others at risk, and remove content that is false or inappropriate.

8. Liability

We work hard to keep records accurate and the service available, but records come from athletes and coaches, and the service is provided "as is". To the extent the law allows, D.A.R.T. is not liable for indirect losses, or for decisions others make based on records.

9. Law

These Terms are governed by the laws of the Republic of Liberia. We will try to resolve any dispute informally first; contact us below.
